About SpeedType

SpeedType is a free typing practice tool designed to help you improve your typing speed and accuracy through timed tests and custom exercises.

Whether you're a beginner looking to learn touch typing or an experienced typist aiming to break speed records, SpeedType provides the tools you need to track your progress and achieve your goals.

Features

  • Multiple test types (timed and page-based)
  • Custom text practice
  • Detailed performance analytics
  • Dark mode and blue light filter
  • Printable certificates
