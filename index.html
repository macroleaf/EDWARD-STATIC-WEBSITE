About Ecliptica Notes

Ecliptica Notes was established in Guruvayur in 2025 with a clear focus: to provide incredibly low-cost essential stationery for the upcoming school opening. We offer a curated selection of 20 essential and important products perfect for students, including quality pens, versatile A4 sheets, practical brown paper, and more – no unnecessary items, just the essentials – all designed to be budget-friendly. Discover attractive offers and equip yourself or your students for the new academic year without compromising on quality.

Please note that this is a static website for the school opening period, showcasing our essential products and attractive offers. To place your order, kindly contact us by calling or WhatsApping +91 95628 98601. We will be accepting orders for the school opening essentials until approximately May 30th. Contact us before this date to secure your supplies of low-cost notebooks and our range of 20 essential school items.
